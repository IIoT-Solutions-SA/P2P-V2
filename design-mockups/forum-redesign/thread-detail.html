Compressed-air loss detection on a three-shift line

FAFiras Al-SiddiqiOperations leadAsked 18 minutes ago

We suspect meaningful compressed-air loss around the carton erectors and sealing stations. The line runs three shifts and we only have short maintenance windows, so a full shutdown survey is not practical this month.

What has worked for teams that need a repeatable survey method, clear leak priority, and a way to verify savings after repairs?

  • Access is restricted around moving conveyors.
  • We can collect compressor power and pressure data from the existing dashboard.
  • The maintenance team wants a prioritized repair list, not just raw leak locations.

Best answerHamad AlharthyMaintenance systems10 min ago

Run a two-pass survey instead of trying to fix everything at once. First, use ultrasonic detection while the line is operating and tag each leak with location, estimated severity, access risk and repair window. Second, validate the highest-priority repairs by comparing compressor kW and pressure stability over the same production period.

The practical ranking formula we use is severity multiplied by accessibility and recurrence risk. That keeps small but easy leaks moving while still surfacing the high-value work that needs a planned stop.
