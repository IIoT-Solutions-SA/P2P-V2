Predictive maintenance

Reducing unplanned downtime with edge vibration monitoring

IIoT Solutions · Riyadh Innovation Center · Riyadh

A practical maintenance improvement

Problem to solve

Limited warning before equipment failures

Unexpected failures on critical rotating equipment were interrupting production and maintenance teams had limited warning before breakdowns. Existing inspection intervals did not always identify faults early enough for planned intervention.

Technology used

Monitoring at the edge

The implementation used wireless vibration sensors, an industrial edge gateway, condition-monitoring dashboards, and anomaly-detection models. The solution prioritized practical alerts instead of sending raw sensor data to maintenance teams.

Vibration sensorsIndustrial edgeAnomaly detectionMaintenance dashboard
Outcomes and results

Earlier warning and better maintenance planning

Maintenance teams gained earlier warning of developing faults, reduced emergency interventions, and improved planning for shutdown work. The organization can now review evidence before deciding when an asset requires inspection or maintenance.

Contributor note: Results were shared as observed operational improvements; no mandatory percentage or ROI calculation was required.

Challenges

Establishing useful baselines

Early challenges included selecting sensor positions, establishing reliable baselines across different operating conditions, and tuning alerts so technicians received useful evidence without unnecessary notifications.
