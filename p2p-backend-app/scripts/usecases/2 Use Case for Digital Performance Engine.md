## **3\. Business Challenge**

**Industry Context**  
 Manufacturing operations require continuous visibility into machine performance to meet tight schedules and high-quality standards. Manual logging of downtime and alarms is error-prone, often causing delays in corrective actions and inefficient resource allocation. Without real-time insights, production leaders face difficulties in quickly identifying and resolving bottlenecks.

**Specific Problems**

1. No real-time visibility of machine efficiency and performance data

2. Delays in identifying and responding to alarms

3. Lack of structured downtime analysis for root-cause resolution

**Financial Loss/Impact**  
 Estimated SAR 1,050,000 annual losses due to production inefficiencies, extended downtimes, and missed optimization opportunities

  ## **8\. Challenges & Solutions**

**Challenge Name**: Alarm Overload

* Description: Operators were initially overwhelmed by frequent alarms without clear prioritization

* Solution: Introduced categorization and auto-assignment of alarms to responsible teams

* Outcome: Faster and more focused alarm resolution

**Challenge Name**: Downtime Logging Discipline

* Description: Operators did not consistently record downtime causes in early rollout

* Solution: Simplified digital input forms and linked them to operator dashboards

* Outcome: Consistent downtime cause reporting achieved within 2 months

  ## **9\. Technical Architecture**

**System Overview**  
 A hybrid IoT and dashboard-based solution for capturing machine data in real time, processing alarms, and visualizing performance metrics.

**Architecture Components**

* Data Layer: IoT sensors, MQTT broker

* Application Layer: Performance engine, alarm management system, downtime analysis tool

* Presentation Layer: Web dashboard, mobile app for notifications

**Security Measures**

* Encrypted communication between IoT devices and cloud servers

* Role-based access controls for operators and supervisors

* Regular penetration testing and vulnerability assessments

**Scalability Design**

* Modular design enabling integration of new machines

* Cloud-based architecture for dynamic scaling

* Load balancing to support multiple concurrent dashboards

  ## **11\. Lessons Learned**

**Category**: Technical  
 **Lesson Title**: Alarm prioritization is critical  
 **Description**: Without prioritization, the system initially created too much noise, making it hard for operators to focus on critical issues.  
 **Recommendation**: Build alarm logic early with input from operators to ensure actionable alerts.
