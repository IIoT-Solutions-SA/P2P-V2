# **Use Case Submission – Energy Consumption Monitoring**

---

## **1\. Basic Information Section**

**Title**  
 Energy Consumption Monitoring

**Subtitle**  
 Granular real-time energy cost tracking improves budgeting and operational planning

**Description/Executive Summary**  
 This use case provides detailed insights into energy consumption for each connected asset. The platform monitors real-time energy usage and calculates the cost in Saudi Riyals per hour for every asset. This granular visibility enables users to identify high-consumption equipment, evaluate operational efficiency, and take corrective actions to optimize energy expenditures. By offering clear cost analysis, the system improves budgeting accuracy and supports proactive operational planning.

## **3\. Business Challenge**

**Industry Context**  
 Energy costs are a significant component of overall production expenses. Without accurate monitoring of asset-level consumption, facilities risk overspending and misallocating resources. Traditional utility bills provide aggregate data, but lack the detail needed to optimize specific machines or processes.

**Specific Problems**

1. No visibility into energy consumption per individual machine

2. Inability to track real-time energy costs in SAR/hour

3. Inefficient budgeting due to reliance on monthly utility summaries

**Financial Loss/Impact**  
 Estimated SAR 675,000 annual overspending from inefficient energy usage and lack of asset-level insights

---

## **4\. Solution Overview**

**Selection Criteria**

1. Real-time energy usage monitoring at machine level

2. Automatic calculation of SAR/hour cost for each asset

3. Dashboards with actionable insights for operators and managers

**Selected Vendor**  
 IIoT Solutions (with KACST)

**Technology Components**

* IoT-enabled smart energy meters for each connected asset

* Cloud analytics platform for cost calculation in SAR/hour

* Dashboards and reporting tools for real-time energy insights

## **8\. Challenges & Solutions**

**Challenge Name**: Data Accuracy from Smart Meters

* Description: Initial smart meters showed inconsistent readings under fluctuating loads

* Solution: Calibrated meters and added load-balancing algorithms in preprocessing

* Outcome: Achieved \>96% accurate readings across all assets

**Challenge Name**: Operator Awareness of Energy Costs

* Description: Operators were not accustomed to monitoring energy cost in SAR/hour

* Solution: Introduced training sessions and integrated cost indicators into dashboards

* Outcome: Increased operator accountability and proactive cost-saving actions

---

## **9\. Technical Architecture**

**System Overview**  
 IoT-enabled energy monitoring architecture that collects per-asset consumption data and converts it into SAR/hour costs for real-time display and analysis.

**Architecture Components**

* Data Layer: IoT smart energy meters on connected assets

* Application Layer: Cloud analytics engine for consumption and cost calculation

* Presentation Layer: Dashboards, mobile reports, and supervisor notifications

**Security Measures**

* Encrypted transmission of energy data from meters to cloud

* Role-based access to dashboards and reports

* Regular audits of meter calibration and system performance

**Scalability Design**

* Modular meter deployment for additional machines

* Cloud scalability for high-frequency energy data

* Expandable dashboards to include multiple facilities
