# **Use Case Submission – Quality Testing Using Digital Vision**

---

## **1\. Basic Information Section**

**Title**  
 Quality Testing Using Digital Vision

**Subtitle**  
 Automated vision inspection improves quality consistency and reduces manual errors

**Description/Executive Summary**  
 This use case utilizes advanced digital vision technology to enhance quality control on the production line. A vision model inspects bottles in real time, analyzing their fill levels as they pass under the camera. Bottles filled to 100% are marked as "Pass," while those below the threshold are marked as "No Pass," triggering an alert. By automating defect detection, this system ensures consistent product standards, reduces manual inspection errors, and streamlines the production process.

## **3\. Business Challenge**

**Industry Context**  
 Consistent product quality is critical in manufacturing. Traditional manual inspection methods are labor-intensive, prone to human error, and unable to guarantee uniform standards at scale. Vision-based systems offer greater reliability and consistency while enabling faster production speeds.

**Specific Problems**

1. Manual bottle inspection prone to inconsistency and human fatigue

2. Inability to maintain uniform quality standards during high-volume production

3. Delayed detection of defects, leading to wasted resources and rework

**Financial Loss/Impact**  
 Estimated SAR 500,000 annual cost impact from defective products, rework, and inefficiencies in manual inspection

## **8\. Challenges & Solutions**

**Challenge Name**: False Negatives in Early Detection

* Description: Early versions of the vision model sometimes misclassified partially filled bottles as pass

* Solution: Retrained model with expanded dataset including edge cases

* Outcome: Achieved 98%+ accuracy in fill-level detection

**Challenge Name**: Integration with Production Workflow

* Description: Initial integration caused brief delays in conveyor speed

* Solution: Optimized data processing pipeline and used edge devices for real-time inference

* Outcome: Seamless inspection with no production delays

## **9\. Technical Architecture**

**System Overview**  
 An AI-driven vision system integrated with production lines to automate real-time quality inspection of bottles.

**Architecture Components**

* Data Layer: High-resolution cameras capturing bottle images

* Application Layer: AI vision model for defect detection, edge computing modules

* Presentation Layer: Operator dashboards, alert notifications

**Security Measures**

* Encrypted storage of inspection data

* Role-based access for supervisors and quality managers

* Regular retraining of AI model to maintain accuracy

**Scalability Design**

* Expandable to additional product types beyond bottles

* Scalable to higher production speeds with parallel camera setups

* Cloud-enabled retraining and monitoring for continuous improvement

## **11\. Lessons Learned**

**Category**: Technical  
 **Lesson Title**: Dataset diversity ensures reliability  
 **Description**: Early training datasets did not cover all defect variations, reducing accuracy. Expanding the dataset significantly improved results.  
 **Recommendation**: Always invest time in curating diverse and representative training datasets for AI vision systems.
