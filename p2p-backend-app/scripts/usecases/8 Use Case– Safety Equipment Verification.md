# **Use Case Submission – Safety Equipment Verification**

---

## **1\. Basic Information Section**

**Title**  
 Safety Equipment Verification

**Subtitle**  
 Vision-based system ensures operator safety compliance before shop floor access

**Description/Executive Summary**  
 This use case introduces an automated vision-based verification system that checks operators for required personal protective equipment (PPE) — such as helmets, vests, and safety gear — before granting access to the shop floor. The system uses cameras and AI models to analyze compliance in real time, displaying a green light for fully compliant operators. This solution enhances safety, reduces risks, and ensures adherence to standards with minimal manual supervision.

## **3\. Business Challenge**

**Industry Context**  
 In manufacturing facilities, strict safety compliance is essential to protect operators and maintain high operational standards. Manual safety checks are time-consuming, inconsistent, and prone to human error. An automated approach ensures accuracy, consistency, and reduced risk of accidents.

**Specific Problems**

1. Delays at entry points due to manual PPE verification

2. Inconsistent enforcement of safety protocols by supervisors

3. Higher risk of accidents from missed PPE checks

**Financial Loss/Impact**  
 Estimated SAR 675,000 annual impact from accidents, downtime, and regulatory non-compliance risks

  ## **8\. Challenges & Solutions**

**Challenge Name**: False Positives in Detection

* Description: Early versions of the AI model sometimes failed to recognize safety vests due to lighting conditions

* Solution: Retrained model with diverse lighting datasets and added infrared support

* Outcome: Achieved 98% detection accuracy across all environments

**Challenge Name**: Operator Acceptance

* Description: Some operators resisted the automated verification, preferring manual checks

* Solution: Conducted awareness sessions highlighting safety and efficiency benefits

* Outcome: Full adoption achieved within 1 month

  ## **9\. Technical Architecture**

**System Overview**  
 AI-based computer vision system integrated with access control gates to verify operator PPE compliance in real time.

**Architecture Components**

* Data Layer: Cameras, image preprocessing module

* Application Layer: AI detection engine, access control integration, compliance dashboard

* Presentation Layer: Web-based monitoring system, operator compliance reports

**Security Measures**

* Secure data handling and encrypted video streams

* Role-based access control for dashboard users

* Regular model validation and system audits

**Scalability Design**

* Modular architecture for adding new PPE types

* Cloud backend capable of scaling with more cameras

* Edge processing to minimize latency at checkpoints

  ## **11\. Lessons Learned**

**Category**: Change Management  
 **Lesson Title**: Early operator engagement reduces resistance  
 **Description**: Initial resistance from operators was due to lack of understanding about the benefits of automated verification.  
 **Recommendation**: Include operators early in training and explain benefits clearly to increase adoption rates.
