# **Use Case Submission – Energy Management System**

---

## **1\. Basic Information Section**

**Title**  
 Energy Management System

**Subtitle**  
 Real-time monitoring and control reduce energy costs and support sustainability goals

**Description/Executive Summary**  
 This use case introduces an Energy Management System designed to optimize energy consumption across factory assets. The system enables operators to set consumption limits per machine, sends real-time alerts when thresholds are exceeded, and provides dashboards for energy analytics. By controlling energy use proactively, the factory reduces costs, avoids overconsumption, and advances its sustainability objectives.

## **3\. Business Challenge**

**Industry Context**  
 Modern manufacturing facilities rely heavily on energy-intensive assets such as CNC machines, 3D printers, and AMRs. Without active monitoring, energy inefficiencies lead to high operational costs, hinder sustainability goals, and create difficulty in managing energy allocation effectively.

**Specific Problems**

1. Lack of visibility into real-time energy usage per asset

2. Frequent overconsumption resulting in higher utility bills

3. Limited ability to align production operations with sustainability targets

**Financial Loss/Impact**  
 Estimated SAR 825,000 annual overspending on energy due to inefficiencies and overuse

## **4\. Solution Overview**

**Selection Criteria**

1. Real-time monitoring and alerting for energy consumption

2. Customizable energy limits per machine or work cell

3. Integration with operator dashboards and mobile notifications

 IIoT Solutions (with KACST)

**Technology Components**

* IoT energy meters and smart plugs for machine-level monitoring

  ## **8\. Challenges & Solutions**

**Challenge Name**: Operator Alert Fatigue

* Description: Too many low-priority alerts initially overwhelmed operators

* Solution: Introduced threshold tiers and prioritized critical alerts

* Outcome: Improved operator response times and reduced alert fatigue

**Challenge Name**: Data Accuracy from Mixed Assets

* Description: Different machine types produced inconsistent energy data

* Solution: Standardized data formats through middleware and sensor calibration

* Outcome: Achieved 95%+ reliable energy data across all monitored assets

  ## **9\. Technical Architecture**

**System Overview**  
 IoT-enabled energy management architecture that integrates energy meters and gateways with a cloud analytics platform to provide real-time monitoring, alerts, and dashboards.

**Architecture Components**

* Data Layer: IoT energy meters, smart plugs, MQTT gateways

* Application Layer: Energy analytics engine, middleware APIs

* Presentation Layer: Web-based dashboard, mobile notification system

**Security Measures**

* Encrypted communication between IoT devices and cloud

* Role-based access control for operators and managers

* Regular compliance checks with ISO 50001 standards

**Scalability Design**

* Modular sensors for additional machines

* Cloud auto-scaling to handle increased data volumes

* Load balancing for high-frequency alert processing

  ## **11\. Lessons Learned**

**Category**: Process  
 **Lesson Title**: Alert prioritization drives adoption  
 **Description**: Early overload of alerts discouraged operators from responding consistently.  
 **Recommendation**: Design tiered alert systems that separate critical events from minor notifications.
