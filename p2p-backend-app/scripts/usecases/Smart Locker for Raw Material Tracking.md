# **Use Case Submission – Smart Locker for Raw Material Tracking**

---

## **1\. Basic Information Section**

**Title**  
 Smart Locker for Raw Material Tracking

**Subtitle**  
 IoT-enabled smart locker with weight scales automates raw material tracking and notifications

**Description/Executive Summary**  
 The Smart Factory implemented an IoT-enabled Smart Locker to manage raw materials with real-time weight detection. Each compartment is equipped with a digital scale to track withdrawals. When an operator removes material, the system records the exact time, material type, and weight, and instantly sends a notification. This ensures full visibility of material usage, prevents shortages, and enables data-driven inventory management.

## **3\. Business Challenge**

**Industry Context**  
 In manufacturing environments, raw materials are critical for uninterrupted production. Traditional material tracking relies on manual logs or periodic inventory checks, which are error-prone and delay replenishment. Lack of real-time visibility leads to production stoppages, excess safety stock, and inefficiencies in material handling.

**Specific Problems**

* No real-time tracking of raw material withdrawals

* High dependency on manual logging by operators

* Frequent material shortages disrupting production

**Financial Loss/Impact**  
 Estimated SAR 400,000 annual cost impact due to downtime, excess stock purchases, and inefficiencies in manual inventory tracking

## **8\. Challenges & Solutions**

**Challenge Name**: Sensor Calibration Drift

* Description: Weight sensors occasionally produced inaccurate readings over time

* Solution: Implemented auto-calibration cycles and alerts for recalibration needs

* Outcome: Achieved \>97% accuracy in weight tracking

**Challenge Name**: Operator Adoption Resistance

* Description: Operators were initially hesitant to rely on automated notifications instead of manual logs

* Solution: Conducted awareness sessions and added real-time dashboards for transparency

* Outcome: Full adoption achieved within 6 weeks

 **Lesson Title**: Calibration is key for accuracy
